# HeatShift — Demo

## Goal

A judge should understand the idea in under 20 seconds.

---

## Opening

"AI infrastructure schedules workloads based on available compute.

But compute exists in the physical world, and the thermal conditions around those facilities change by location and time.

HeatShift makes temperature part of the scheduling decision."

---

## Demo Flow

### 1. Show Infrastructure

Display several compute sites on a map.

Display pending workloads.

---

### 2. Show Thermal Intelligence

Move the time slider.

Thermal conditions around the sites change.

Message:

"The best location to run flexible compute can change over the course of the day."

---

### 3. Run Baseline

Click:

RUN BASELINE

Display:

- workloads completed
- deadlines met
- GPU-hours
- estimated energy
- estimated operating cost

---

### 4. Run HeatShift

Click:

OPTIMIZE WITH HEATSHIFT

Animate workloads moving between eligible sites/time windows.

---

### 5. Compare

Show:

BASELINE | HEATSHIFT

Highlight:

- same required work
- deadlines preserved
- calculated energy difference
- calculated cost difference

Never hard-code the improvement.

---

### 6. Explain One Move

Click a workload.

Show:

Baseline assignment
HeatShift assignment
Thermal difference
Cost difference
Reason

---

## Closing

"HeatShift doesn't reduce the amount of useful compute.

It makes the physical location and timing of that compute smarter.

Move compute. Not heat."
