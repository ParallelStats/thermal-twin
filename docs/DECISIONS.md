# HeatShift — Decision Log

## 2026-08-18 — Product Direction

Decision:
Build HeatShift.

Reason:
It combines a non-obvious use of hyperlocal thermal intelligence with a measurable economic outcome and a technically demonstrable optimization problem.

---

## 2026-08-18 — Core Architecture

Decision:
Use Next.js frontend + FastAPI backend + Python OR-Tools optimizer.

Reason:
Simple architecture, strong visualization capabilities, and mature optimization tooling.

---

## 2026-08-18 — Numerical Integrity

Decision:
All savings displayed by the product must be calculated.

Reason:
The project's credibility depends on demonstrating an actual optimization outcome rather than fabricated impact.

---

## 2026-08-18 — MVP Scope

Decision:
Use simulated compute infrastructure/workloads with real or cached thermal intelligence.

Reason:
A functioning scheduling product can be demonstrated without access to a real GPU fleet.
